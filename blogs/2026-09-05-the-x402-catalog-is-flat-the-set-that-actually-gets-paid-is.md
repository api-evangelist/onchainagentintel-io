The x402 endpoint market has two shapes at once, and read together they explain more than either alone. The Coinbase CDP Bazaar — the largest single public x402 discovery catalog on Base — is not accumulating items on net over the ~20-day rolling-window span the in-repo daily snapshot archive supports. The subset that has actually crossed the ≥5-distinct-paying-wallets-in-30-days bar is growing much faster than the catalog: 563 → 727 endpoints (+29.13%), with 72.82% of the earlier cohort persisting into the current window and 317 net new entrants across the 5-payer bar.
