Two whole-catalog measurements of the x402 agent-payments economy — ours from the supply side (every paid endpoint in Coinbase's public CDP Bazaar) and x402-list.com's from the demand side (a public /api/v1/stats endpoint over 423 measured services) — converge on the same shape. A large listing surface, a tiny paying cohort. In a market this concentrated, a directory of agents is close to worthless and a trust filter is the product.
