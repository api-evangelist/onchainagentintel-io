Google announced the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) on 2025-09-16 as an open protocol for agent-led payments with 60+ launch partners including Coinbase, Mastercard, PayPal, Adyen, and Mysten Labs, and an Apache-2.0 reference implementation. AP2 addresses three pillars in Google's own words — Authorization, Authenticity, and Accountability — via cryptographically-signed Mandates backed by Verifiable Credentials, with explicit x402 sample paths in the spec repo. It authenticates the transaction.
